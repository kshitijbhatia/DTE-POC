# Data card: Battery and Heating Data in Real Driving Cycles (BMW i3)

> Generated by `src/data/data_card.py`; every number below is computed from the data. Re-run `python -m src.data.build_dataset && python -m src.data.data_card` to regenerate.

## Source

- **Dataset:** *Battery and Heating Data in Real Driving Cycles*, IEEE DataPort (open access): https://ieee-dataport.org/open-access/battery-and-heating-data-real-driving-cycles
- **Vehicle:** BMW i3 (battery electric), driven on public roads around Munich.
- **Files:** one CSV per trip (`TripA01`–`TripA32`, `TripB01`–`TripB38`) plus `Overview.xlsx` (date, route, weather, notes per trip). Semicolon-separated, Latin-1 encoded, CRLF line endings.

## Raw file quirks handled by the loader

- 6 distinct header layouts across 70 files.
- `TripB38` spells speed as `Velocity [km/h]]]`; `Regenerative Braking Signal ` has a trailing space; `max. SoC [%)` has a bracket typo (kept as-is).
- `TripA11` has an extra empty column; `TripA21` lacks `Requested Coolant Temperature`.
- Series B lists `Temperature Vent right [°C]` twice (one with a trailing space). Dropped anyway.
- 27 trips (all in series B) have missing SoC values, mostly the last 1–2 rows.

## Cleaning steps

Implemented in `src/data/clean.py`, tested in `tests/test_clean.py`. A row is counted under the first step that flags it.

| step | problem and rule | action | rows | trips affected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `interpolated` | Speed **and** current form perfect straight lines (up to 2-decimal rounding) for ≥ 5 s while moving: a logging gap filled by linear interpolation. In TripB02 this block contains throttle 135 % and heater 40 kW, both physically impossible. | drop | 6,007 | TripB02, TripB34 |
| `soc_glitch` | SoC jumps > 1 %-point between consecutive 0.1 s samples (real steps are ≤ 0.06). TripB14's last row reads 0 % after 34.6 %. | drop | 1 | TripB14 |
| `soc_frozen` | SoC unchanged ≥ 120 s while mean absolute current > 10 A: a frozen BMS signal. TripB07's SoC sticks at 50.4 % while the car keeps drawing current, then goes missing. | drop | 3,032 | TripB07 |
| `temp_spike` | Battery temperature > 5 °C away from its 1 s rolling median. TripA10's first row reads 60.9 °C max battery temperature, the next row 23.0 °C. | drop | 1 | TripA10 |
| `charging` | Speed < 1 km/h with current > +10 A for ≥ 60 s: a charging stop (Overview notes fast charging in B04 and B09). Not driving, and it raises SoC. | drop; trip splits into segments | 39,415 | TripB04, TripB09 |
| `soc_missing` | SoC is NaN. SoC is needed for the target, so these rows are unusable. | drop | 5,845 | TripB05, TripB06, TripB07, TripB08, TripB10, TripB11, TripB12, TripB13, TripB18, TripB19, TripB20, TripB21, TripB22, TripB23, TripB24, TripB25, TripB26, TripB28, TripB29, TripB30, TripB31, TripB32, TripB35, TripB36, TripB37 |
| `short_segment` | A piece of trip < 60 s left between dropped rows. | drop | 393 | TripB02 |

Column-level fix: `heater_power_kw` set to missing in 3 trips (TripA05, TripA06, TripA13) that fail the heater plausibility check (see Sanity checks). Their rows are kept.

Not done (deliberately): no outlier clipping (after the steps above every signal is within physical limits; see min/max in the column table), no resampling (10 Hz is kept), no imputation of missing SoC.

## Sanity checks

- **Current sign.** During TripB04's charging stop the current is positive (median 50.8 A) and SoC rises, so negative current = discharge.
- **SoC tracks integrated current (coulomb counting).** Across 69 trip segments with ≥ 2 %-points of SoC drop, 1 %-point of SoC = 0.617 Ah (IQR 0.616–0.619) = 0.2282 kWh (IQR 0.2212–0.2322). Implied gross capacity: ≈ 62 Ah / 22.8 kWh, consistent with the 60 Ah BMW i3 (our inference; the dataset does not state the battery version).
- **Coulomb drift.** Re-computing SoC from integrated current, the BMS SoC stays within 0.08 %-points (median over segments; 90th pct 0.10) of it. Segments drifting > 1.5 points: TripB07 seg 0 (4.4), TripA06 seg 0 (1.9), TripB04 seg 1 (1.6). TripB07 is the trip whose SoC later freezes; its SoC is less trustworthy than the rest.
- **Heater power plausibility.** Standing still, the battery only feeds auxiliaries, so heater power should be ≤ battery draw. Of the 65 trips with ≥ 30 s of standstill, it fails in TripA06 (heater 5.32 kW vs battery 1.13 kW), TripA05 (heater 1.52 kW vs battery 0.83 kW), TripA13 (heater 1.48 kW vs battery 0.90 kW). TripA06 reports ~5.3 kW of heating for its whole duration at ~30 °C ambient, a stuck signal; it alone was 52% of all heater energy reported in series A. **Action:** `heater_power_kw` is set to missing for these trips (rows kept; other signals are fine).
- **Distance.** For the 64 trips the cleaning left essentially intact, integrated speed matches Overview.xlsx distance within 0.1 km for 63 of them (median difference 0.000 km). Exceptions: TripA30 (10.73 km in CSV vs 7.75 km in Overview). TripA30's Overview row also shares its start time with TripA31, and its duration matches the CSV, so we treat the Overview distance as the error.
- **Acceleration sensor** vs. differentiated speed: correlation 0.94.
- **Displayed SoC** is close to the BMS SoC rescaled onto its usable window (`(soc − soc_min) / (soc_max − soc_min) × 100`) but not exactly: the dashboard value is on average 3.8 points higher (std 2.0, max |diff| 10.1). The exact mapping is not documented.
- **Overview.xlsx inconsistency.** TripA31 lists SoC 0.810 → 0.825 (an increase) with a SoC-difference of 0.719; the CSV shows 81.0 → 71.9 %. We use the CSV values.

## Open questions

- Which SoC defines "empty" for distance-to-empty: BMS SoC reaching `soc_min` (8 %) or displayed SoC reaching 0? Matters in Phase 2.
- Elevation source (GPS vs. barometric) and the `regen_signal` scale are undocumented.
- Some trips carry extra payload (+60 to +160 kg, noted in Overview.xlsx); payload is not a signal in the CSVs.
